Pints for a Purpose

What happens when you fill a brewery with generous hearts, good humans, and incredible raffle prizes? A whole lot of JOY—and a community showing up for local families!

Pints for a Purpose is our annual fundraiser and one of our favorite ways to bring people together. There’s something incredibly beautiful about good, kind, accepting people coming together to make a difference in their community. That kind of energy is contagious. So is joy—and we love seeing it ripple beyond the event and inspire even more kindness!

At the heart of it all is raising funds to support local families of individuals with disabilities. Every donation earns you raffle tickets, and you choose which prizes you want to try to win! Everyone who gives gets to share in the excitement, and every contribution helps us do more through Spread Joy Collective. Every person matters. Every donation matters. And when all that generosity comes together, the ripple of joy keeps growing! 💛
